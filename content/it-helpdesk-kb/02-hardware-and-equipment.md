# Hardware & Equipment — Cobalt Dynamics IT Helpdesk

*Invented content for a student project — no real inventory, no real staff.*

## Standard issue
- New hires receive a laptop (14" business-class, Windows or macOS per role), a headset, and a
  monitor if working from a Cobalt Dynamics office.
- Replacement cycle: laptops are refreshed every 3 years, or sooner if faulty.

## Requesting new or replacement hardware
- Open a helpdesk ticket under "Hardware & Equipment". Include: item needed, business reason,
  manager name.
- Standard hardware: approved same-day, delivered within 5 business days.
- Non-standard hardware (e.g. a second monitor, specialised peripherals): requires manager budget
  sign-off, adds 2–3 business days.

## Loaner equipment
- Travelling or waiting on a repair? Loaner laptops are available from the IT cage at each office;
  reserve one at least 1 business day ahead via the same ticket category.

## Returning equipment
- All Cobalt Dynamics hardware must be returned at offboarding (see `04-onboarding-and-offboarding.md`)
  or when replaced. IT wipes and re-images returned devices before reissue.

## Common faults and first steps
- Laptop won't turn on: confirm charger LED is lit; try a different outlet; hold power 15s to
  force-restart before logging a ticket.
- Docking station not detected: unplug/replug the dock's power and USB-C cable; Windows users,
  check Device Manager for a yellow-warning entry.
