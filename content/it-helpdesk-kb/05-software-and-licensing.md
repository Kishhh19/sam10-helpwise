# Software & Licensing — Cobalt Dynamics IT Helpdesk

*Invented content for a student project.*

## Installing software
- Most staff can self-install anything listed in the internal Software Catalogue — no ticket
  needed.
- Software not in the catalogue requires a ticket under "Software & Licensing" with business
  justification; IT and Security review before approval (typically 2–3 business days, longer if
  it needs a security/data review).

## Licensing
- Named-user licences (e.g. design or analytics tools) are assigned per request and reclaimed
  automatically 30 days after an account is deactivated.
- Shared/floating licences are checked out per session by the tool itself; if you're blocked by a
  "no licences available" message, try again later or ask your manager whether more seats are
  needed.

## Browser extensions and personal tools
- Browser extensions from outside the approved list, and any personal (non-work) cloud storage or
  messaging tool, are blocked on company devices by policy — this is intentional, not a fault.

## Common issues
- "License server unreachable": usually a VPN/network issue — see `03-network-and-vpn.md` first.
- App won't update: company devices apply software updates on a managed schedule; you can request
  an out-of-cycle update via a ticket if it's blocking real work.
