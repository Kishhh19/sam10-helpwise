# Security Incidents — Cobalt Dynamics IT Helpdesk

*Invented content for a student project. This document is informational — it tells staff what to
do and who to contact. It does not make decisions about any person, and HelpWise does not take
any incident-response action itself; it only surfaces this guidance.*

## If you suspect your account is compromised
1. Do not wait for a ticket queue — call the Security Hotline (internal extension 4100) or email
   `security@cobaltdynamics.example` immediately.
2. If you still have access, change your CD-SSO password immediately from a trusted device.
3. Security will walk you through re-securing MFA and reviewing recent account activity.

## If you suspect phishing
- Forward the suspicious email as an attachment to `phishing@cobaltdynamics.example` — do not
  click links or reply to the sender first.
- Do not forward it to colleagues "to warn them" — Security broadcasts a warning if needed, to
  avoid spreading the link further.

## If you lose a company device
- Report immediately via the Security Hotline, any time of day — do not wait for business hours.
  Devices are encrypted and remotely wipeable, but faster reporting means a faster remote wipe.

## What this assistant cannot do
- HelpWise cannot unlock accounts, approve access, or confirm whether a specific incident has been
  contained — those require a human on the Security team. It can only tell you the right process
  and contact to use.
