# Account & Access — Cobalt Dynamics IT Helpdesk

*All names, systems and policies below are invented for this student project. Cobalt Dynamics is
not a real company.*

## Password resets
- Staff accounts use Cobalt Dynamics Single Sign-On (CD-SSO). To reset a forgotten password, go to
  `sso.cobaltdynamics.example/reset` and follow the email verification link.
- Passwords must be at least 14 characters and are rotated every 180 days.
- Locked out after 5 failed attempts? The lock clears automatically after 30 minutes, or ask IT to
  clear it immediately via a helpdesk ticket.

## Multi-factor authentication (MFA)
- MFA is mandatory for all staff on CD-SSO, using the Cobalt Authenticator app.
- Lost your MFA device? Submit a helpdesk ticket under "Account & Access > Lost MFA device" with
  your employee ID; IT will verify your identity over a callback to your registered number before
  re-enrolling a new device.

## New system access requests
- Access to shared drives, internal tools, or customer-data systems requires manager approval,
  logged through the Access Request Portal.
- Standard turnaround: 1 business day for internal tools, 3 business days for customer-data systems
  (these require a security review).

## Who to contact
- Routine access issues: helpdesk ticket, category "Account & Access"
- Suspected compromised account: escalate immediately — see `06-security-incidents.md`
